About Me

Rooted in Oklahoma City, built on people.

I'm an Oklahoma City native with a deep love for my community. I believe that when you invest in your community, it gives back, and that's where my passion for Oklahoma City businesses comes from.

I'm people-oriented at heart. The best understanding and the strongest connections come from time spent together, so while my work can always be done remotely, I never want a client to miss the chance to meet, film, or collaborate with me in person.

Working in this industry, you have to be comfortable being uncomfortable. That's why I've chosen a career that lets me work in so many different spaces, from bars and event planning to agencies and medical practices. Every one is a chance to meet new people and gain new experiences, and every one makes my work better.
