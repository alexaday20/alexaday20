05 / Campaign Pitch · Team Project

Phillips 66

Influencer strategyUGC campaignAudience research
Challenge
Phillips 66 is celebrating the Route 66 Centennial and wanted to connect the road's nostalgia with its modern brands (Phillips 66, Conoco, and 76) while driving awareness and app downloads.
Research
Our survey of 422 travelers showed convenience drives the decision: 282 said convenient locations would bring them to a Phillips 66 brand, and 116 pointed to a positive past experience.
My Role
I created the #FuelYourJourneyWithPhillips66 challenge: travelers post road trip content with the hashtag, top entries are featured on Phillips 66, 76, and Conoco socials, and winners earn fuel discounts. I also built the influencer strategy to amplify it with travel creators.
Projected Results
Travel creator partnerships to spread the challenge, user-generated content built on social proof to grow trust and engagement, and paid social across YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Pitched to the Phillips 66 marketing team.
